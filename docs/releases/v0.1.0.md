# Business Ops Workbench v0.1.0

## Overview

First complete local analysis-engine release, covering validated data ingestion, configurable metrics, business anomaly detection, arithmetic contribution, and structured diagnosis.

## Completed

- M1 — Data Foundation: CSV/XLSX ingestion, mapping, standardization, data quality, DuckDB persistence, metadata, and field availability.
- M2 — Metric Engine: configurable metrics, availability, filters, daily trends, comparisons, rolling windows, and configured funnel analysis.
- M3 — Diagnostics Engine: business anomaly detection, additive contribution analysis, and controlled one-level structured diagnosis.

## Architecture

```text
CSV/XLSX
   ↓
Mapping
   ↓
Standardization
   ↓
DuckDB
   ↓
Metrics
   ↓
Anomaly
   ↓
Contribution
   ↓
Diagnosis
```

## Validation

- Automated tests: 152 passed.
- Reproducible Demo: 109,500 rows across 365 days.
- Performance summary: 365-day trend and funnel queries complete in about 40–60 ms; a full-year anomaly scan in about 0.75 s; channel and salesperson contribution in about 0.16 s; complete event diagnosis in about 1.1–1.5 s on the release workstation.

## Limitations

- This release is not a BI system.
- It is not AI diagnosis or an automated root-cause system.
- It is not a prediction system.
- Real business onboarding still requires confirmed field mapping, metric and threshold calibration, and a business-specific adapter.
